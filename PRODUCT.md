# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Individuals and their spouses/wives preparing in case of incapacity or death. The context of use is serious, reflective, and emotionally taxing. Users need reassurance, high readability, and absolute simplicity.

## Product Purpose
To consolidate all critical life, financial, medical, and final arrangement details not contained in a legal Will, allowing families to manage affairs in an emergency. The packet must be offline-first for privacy, simple to complete, and print-optimized.

## Brand Personality
Trustworthy, dignified, calm.

## Anti-references
SaaS analytics dashboards, glowing dark themes with neon accents, gamified progress indicators, and generic card-grid layouts.

## Design Principles
1. **Sovereign Privacy**: Reassure the user continuously that their sensitive data is stored client-side and never sent to a server.
2. **Quiet Dignity**: Visual presentation should feel calm, respectful, and classic, akin to fine stationery or a premium binder, rather than a tech startup tool.
3. **Seamless Physical Translation**: The printed output must be formatted like a professional legal or typed record, not a printout of a webpage.
4. **Gradual Disclosure**: Prevent cognitive overload by presenting fields and tips logically without overwhelming the screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High color contrast (minimum WCAG AA compliance, text contrast >= 4.5:1).
- Clear, readable typography for users of all ages, including elderly executors.
- Plain, compassionate language (no legal jargon or technical buzzwords).
